# Code of Conduct

Genji Ball is a community project. Keep it a place people want to be.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree about balance or design.
- Give constructive feedback on PRs. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started by not knowing how the Workshop works.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Posting someone's private information
- Taking credit for other people's work
- Persistent trolling or derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers of the Genji Ball Team organization can edit or remove comments, close issues and PRs, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact a maintainer on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/genjiball).

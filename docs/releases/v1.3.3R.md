**Ranked is back.** v1.3.3R is the Ranked version of Genji Ball CE: v1.3.3 free-for-all, with every match logged for the ranked leaderboard at [genjiball.us](https://genjiball.us) and rank tags over the top players. It plays exactly like v1.3.3 `Default`.

Looking for casual free-for-all? That's [v1.3.3](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/releases/tag/1.3.3). Team Deathmatch is [v1.3.3T](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/releases/tag/1.3.3T).

## How a ranked match works
- The lobby is set up for ranked: Deathmatch (FFA) on Workshop Island Night, up to 10 players. Leave the Workshop settings at their defaults.
- The game writes the match to the Workshop log file: who joined and left, each round's elimination order and winner, kills and deflects. The host tool uploads it to genjiball.us when the match ends, and each round moves the ratings.
- Anything that isn't the ranked setup (another map or mode, a preset other than Default, a feel setting, an add-on like duels or sandbox, a bot) makes the match unranked. Everyone sees **"UNRANKED: this match won't count"** at the top of the screen, with the reason, so it's fixed before the match is played.

## Rank tags
- Players in the top tiers get a tag over their head: **Apprentice** (1300), **Master** (1600), **Grandmaster** (1900), **Ascendant** (2200), **Champion** (2500) and **God** (2800). A new player starts at 1000.
- A tier guide on the right links the live leaderboard. Simple HUD (Interact) hides it.
- The names come from the ranked server when the host builds the code, so get a new code when the ranks update.

## New for hosts: 70 - Ranked
- **ranked logging** (on): logs the match for the leaderboard. Also turn on **Enable Workshop Inspector Log File** in the Overwatch options, so the log reaches a file.
- **rank tags** (on) and **tier guide** (on): turn the tags or the guide off.
- **rank tag height** (1.5 m) and **rank tag size** (1): where the tag sits and how big it is.
- **ranked debug HUD** (off): shows the host the ranked log's state (logging, round, players, unranked reasons) without opening the Workshop inspector.

Every setting is in [docs/hosting.md](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/blob/v1.3.3R/docs/hosting.md#ranked-lobbies). The log format is [docs/ranked-log.md](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/blob/v1.3.3R/docs/ranked-log.md).

## Compared to v1.3.2 RANKED
- The log has every round with its full finishing order and winner, plus joins and leaves. v1.3.2 RANKED only logged kills.
- Players are told apart by an id per match, not only by name.
- The unranked warning tells everyone when a match won't count.
- The data center is the host's choice (v1.3.2 RANKED forced Netherlands).
- Plus every fix from [v1.3.3](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/releases/tag/1.3.3).

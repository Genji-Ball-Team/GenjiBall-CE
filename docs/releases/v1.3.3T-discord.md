Everything from v1.3.3, played 5v5. The rules follow zSh4d0W's 1.3T.

**How it plays**
- Deflect the ball at the other team
- A team wins the round when the other team is wiped out
- 1v1 final duel when one player is left on each team
- First to 15 wins, or play on match time

**Passing** (host setting, off by default)
- Hold secondary fire and deflect with ability 2 to pass to a teammate
- A pass costs 100 health; a normal deflect heals it back
- Optional dash passing

**Fixed compared to 1.3T**
- Ties at time-up go to extra rounds (they used to give team 2 the win)
- The game waits for players when a team is empty
- Tournament mode counts every round

Free-for-all stays on v1.3.3 (`926FG`).

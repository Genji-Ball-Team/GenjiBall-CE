**Team Deathmatch is back.** v1.3.3T is the Teams version of Genji Ball CE: everything from v1.3.3, played as two teams of up to 5. The rules follow zSh4d0W's **1.3T**, the Teams version people played before.

Looking for free-for-all? That's [v1.3.3](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/releases/tag/1.3.3).

## Install
**Import code: `11M60`**. In Overwatch, go to Play → Custom Games → Import Code.

Or paste the code yourself:
1. Download **genjiball-v1.3.3T.txt** below and copy its contents.
2. In Overwatch: Custom Games → Create → Settings → Import (top right). Use a new custom game.

Workshop page: [workshop.codes/GenjiBall-CE-Teams](https://workshop.codes/GenjiBall-CE-Teams)

## How Teams plays
- Team Deathmatch, 5v5, on Workshop Island Night.
- A deflect sends the ball at the **other team**: the opponent closest to your crosshair becomes the target.
- A team wins the round when **no one on the other team is left alive**. With one player left on each team, it's a 1v1 final duel.
- The first team to **15** round wins takes the match. Tied when time runs out? Rounds continue until one team leads.
- If a team is empty, the game waits for players and the match timer pauses.

## New for hosts: 70 - Teams
- **score to win** (15): set it to 0 to play until the match time runs out.
- **passing** (off): hold secondary fire and deflect with ability 2 to pass to a teammate. A pass costs 100 health, and a normal deflect at the other team heals it back. With too little health the pass fails. With passing on, secondary fire alone doesn't deflect.
- **dash passing** (off): with passing on, holding secondary fire while dash-hitting the ball also passes.

Every v1.3.3 setting and preset works here too. See [docs/hosting.md](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/blob/v1.3.3T/docs/hosting.md) and [docs/playing.md](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/blob/v1.3.3T/docs/playing.md#teams).

## Fixed compared to 1.3T
- A tie when time ran out gave team 2 the win, and a team 2 lead ended in a draw. The leading team now wins, and a tie goes to extra rounds.
- A round could start, or keep going, with one team empty. The game now waits for players when either team is empty, and the match timer resumes when it starts again.
- Tournament mode now counts every Team Deathmatch round.
- Score to win 0 ended the game straight away. It now plays on match time.
- Plus every fix from [v1.3.3](https://github.com/Genji-Ball-Team/GenjiBall-CE/releases/tag/1.3.3).
